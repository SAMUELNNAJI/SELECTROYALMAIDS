Common Questions

Before reaching out, you might find your answer here.

How quickly will I get a response?

We respond to emails and WhatsApp messages within 2 business hours during office hours. Phone calls are answered immediately Monday–Friday.

Can I visit your office in person?

Yes! Our Lagos and Abuja offices are open Monday–Saturday. We recommend booking an appointment in advance so we can give you dedicated attention.

I'm outside Lagos — can you still help?

Absolutely. We serve families across 15+ Nigerian cities. All consultations can be done remotely via WhatsApp, phone, or video call at no extra charge.

What if I have a complaint about a maid?

Please contact our support team immediately by phone or through the Help & Support section in your account dashboard. We take all complaints seriously and resolve them within 48 hours.

How do I request a replacement?

Contact us within the guarantee period and we will begin sourcing a replacement immediately — free of charge — with no questions asked.

I want to register as a maid — who do I contact?

Email us at register@selectroyalmaids.ng or send us a WhatsApp message. Our team will guide you through the registration and verification process.
