Overview

At SelectRoyal Maids, we are committed to providing a service you can trust. Our refund policy is designed to be fair to both employers and domestic professionals while protecting the integrity of the placement process.

All fees paid to SelectRoyal Maids are for our placement and intermediary services — not for the employment of any individual. The terms below govern when refunds or replacements are available.

In most cases, we offer a free replacement before a monetary refund. A replacement is typically faster and more practical for employers needing immediate help.

Replacement Guarantee

All placements made through SelectRoyal Maids come with our Replacement Guarantee. If your hired candidate does not work out within the guarantee period, we will find you a suitable replacement at no additional charge.

The guarantee period begins on the candidate's first official day of employment. To activate the replacement guarantee, you must notify us within the period above.

Eligible Refund Scenarios

A full or partial monetary refund may be issued in the following circumstances:

  • No Suitable Replacement Found — If we are unable to provide a suitable replacement within 21 business days after you activate the guarantee.
  • Candidate Misrepresentation — If a candidate is found to have materially misrepresented their identity, qualifications, or experience and we cannot provide a replacement.
  • Failed Verification — If a candidate passes our screening but is later found to have a serious undisclosed criminal record that we failed to detect.
  • Duplicate Payment — If you were charged more than once for the same placement due to a system error.
  • Cancelled Before Match — If you paid a placement fee and we have not yet matched you with any candidates, a full refund is available within 7 days of payment.

Non-Refundable Situations

Refunds will not be issued in the following circumstances:

  • The guarantee period has expired at the time of the complaint.
  • The candidate was dismissed due to personal disagreements, personality conflicts, or changed household circumstances unrelated to performance.
  • The employer changed their requirements after placement (e.g. different duties, hours, or location).
  • The employer failed to provide reasonable working conditions as agreed.
  • The complaint was not submitted through our official support channels.
  • The placement fee was paid more than 90 days ago.
  • The employer violated the candidate's rights or our Terms of Service.

Platform subscription fees, consultation fees, and profile promotion fees are non-refundable once services have been rendered.

How to Request a Refund

To request a refund or activate the replacement guarantee, follow these steps:

  1. Contact our support team within the applicable guarantee period.
  2. Provide your account email, placement reference number, and a clear description of the issue.
  3. Our team will review your request within 3 business days.
  4. If a replacement is appropriate, we will begin sourcing within 5 business days.
  5. If a monetary refund is approved, it will be processed within the timeline below.

You can reach our support team via:

Partial Refunds

In certain situations, we may issue a partial refund based on the proportion of service already rendered. For example:

  • If you activate the replacement guarantee but a replacement is found, no monetary refund is issued — the replacement is your remedy.
  • If you cancel a multi-placement package after one placement has been completed, a partial refund may apply to the unused portion.
  • Partial refund amounts are calculated at our discretion based on the circumstances and value of services already provided.

Disputes

If you disagree with a refund decision made by our team, you may escalate the matter as follows:

  • Submit a formal dispute in writing to disputes@selectroyalmaids.ng within 14 days of the initial decision.
  • Our senior management team will review the dispute within 10 business days.
  • If still unresolved, the dispute may be referred to mediation under Nigerian law.

We are committed to resolving all disputes fairly and promptly. Our goal is always a satisfactory outcome for both parties.
